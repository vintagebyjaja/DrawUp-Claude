## Scope and dimensions

Buildings remain at their saved footprints, sizes and orientations. Finch and 50th use the original saved public-road outlines. Site paving and parking respond to the existing curved Finch edge. Upper/lower east parking banks have direct paved access; no grass sits between the connector parking bays and their aisle. Lawn strips around the main school are reduced and a north-side row is added. The west campus drive follows the fixed building geometry more closely.

Main campus drives: 24 feet. Far-left campus drive: 14 feet. One-way connector throat: 11 feet. Angled parking aisles: 13 feet. Typical rectangular stalls: 9 by 18 feet. Vehicle swept paths and final pedestrian/accessibility layouts are not certified by these 2D drawings.

## Mixed central lot — important fit distinction

Options 1 and 3 follow the supplied SECTION, not the spacing in the illustrative graphic: **18 + 18 + 13 + 18 + 18 + 13 + 18 + 18 = 134 feet**, within a 60-foot depth. Back-to-back banks touch directly. No grass strip, separator, or extra pavement band is inserted between adjacent banks. Two 13-foot aisles remain exactly where dimensioned.

28 provisional outlines are shown: six in each perpendicular end bay and four in each of four angled banks. This is a SCHEMATIC count, not a verified 28-stall construction layout. A true 9 by 18 foot rectangular stall rotated 45 degrees projects 19.09 feet across the bank, conflicting with the diagram's 18-foot band. The central angled corner outlines are clipped diagrammatic markings and dashed deliberately; they are not claimed to be full 9 by 18 foot rectangles. The fixed section is preserved as requested. Full angled-stall fit remains to be resolved before 3D modeling; reducing count alone does not resolve that transverse mismatch. Other stall outlines are full-size rectangles.

Option 2 uses full-size perpendicular central stalls; its two back-to-back 18-foot rows occupy 36 feet, with a 24-foot approach giving a 60-foot module. The separate through-site corridor remains outside the parking rows.
