# DrawUp V16.3 — Campus Network

## Added
- Regular Student plan: $20/month.
- Campus Ambassador plan: $10/month (half price) with the same Student benefits.
- Students retain personal DrawUp profiles.
- Each university can have an official DrawUp Campus portal.
- Campus Managers can maintain school-facing information, leadership/chair information, campus posts and approved organizations for their own university.
- Students can create organizations under their school for verification/approval.
- Organization Managers are scoped only to the organization(s) they are authorized to manage.
- Supported organization model includes AIAS, NOMAS, NSBE and other approved campus organizations.
- Official photos/media can be published only by authorized Organization Managers on organization pages or Campus Managers on university portals.
- Regular students can submit projects/content for consideration without gaining campus publishing authority.
- Public DrawUp Universities timeline aggregates approved public campus and organization posts so students and the public can see what is happening across universities.
- Verified badge UI for approved campus/organization publishing identities.

## Security boundary
`owner_admin` is private/internal only. Campus and organization roles never grant DrawUp owner/admin access.

Role scopes:
- `student`: own account/profile.
- `organization_manager`: assigned organization only.
- `campus_manager`: assigned university portal only.
- `lead_campus_manager`: assigned university portal with higher campus permissions.
- `owner_admin`: private DrawUp internal backend only.

All production authorization must be enforced server-side/RLS, not merely by hiding UI controls.
